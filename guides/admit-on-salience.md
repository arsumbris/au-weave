# Admit on salience

Admit a candidate for what it contributes within the premise.
Check that the source supports a candidate of the selected kind.
Use that kind's guidance to distinguish it from an incidental phrase.

**Judge the contribution**

Apply the premise's intent and explicit exclusions.
Within that scope, weigh the candidate using the domain's admission guidance:

- A source focused on the candidate can justify a node
- Distinct sources adding knowledge can strengthen the case
- A candidate can fill a gap the premise needs
- Other contributions may qualify under the domain's guidance

Recurrence does not override an exclusion.
A low source count does not disqualify a candidate.

**Record the decision**

Record the contribution or gap in the cut's `why`.
Park an unresolved judgment.
Drop a rejected candidate with its reason.
